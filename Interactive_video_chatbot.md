# DAY 1 — NODE-BY-NODE DECISION TREE

**Mission Goal:** Safety, trust, movement literacy, expectations
**Hidden Goal:** Teach the client *how to think* about training

---

## NODE 0 — ENTRY STATE (Silent Intake)

**Triggered when user starts Day 1**

**State variables initialized:**

* Confidence = unknown
* Experience = unknown
* Pain/Injury = unknown
* Coaching Density = high (default)
* Autonomy = low (default)

⬇️ auto-advance

---

## NODE 1 — GREETING / FRAME THE SESSION (VIDEO)

🎥 *“Here’s what today is about. No pressure. We’re learning.”*

**Purpose**

* Lower anxiety
* Set expectations
* Establish authority without intimidation

**Implicit questions asked (can be explicit or chat-based):**

* Have you trained before?
* Any injuries or pain today?
* How are you feeling about starting?

**Branch outcomes (DO NOT change workout):**

* Nervous → slower pacing, more reassurance
* Confident → tighter explanations
* Injury flagged → extra form reminders later

⬇️

---

## NODE 2 — MYOFASCIAL RELEASE (UNIVERSAL)

🎥 *“Everyone starts here, regardless of age or ability.”*

**Actions**

* Foam roll / ball / gentle tissue work
* Emphasis on sensation, not pain

**Decision check (chat or quick input):**

* “Any sharp pain?”

  * YES → mark Pain = true
  * NO → continue

**State impact**

* If pain = true → coaching density stays high
* If no pain → coaching density can reduce later

⬇️

---

## NODE 3 — FIRST MOVEMENT WARM-UP (ASSESSMENT DISGUISED)

🎥 *“This isn’t about warming up. It’s about how you move.”*

**Movements**

* Bracing
* Breathing
* Basic joint control
* Slow tempo

**Decision checks**

* Can they maintain posture?
* Can they breathe under control?
* Can they follow cues?

**Branch**

* Struggles → flag Form = needs work
* Adequate → Form = acceptable
* Strong → Form = solid

**State impact**

* Form flag affects cueing later, not exercise choice

⬇️

---

## NODE 4 — WARM-UP STYLE SELECTION (COACH-CHOSEN)

🎥 *“Here’s why we’re doing THIS warm-up today.”*

**Options (system chooses):**

* Upper body
* Lower body
* Med ball
* Bodyweight
* Athletic
* Ladder

**Decision logic (hidden):**

* Pain flagged? → simpler pattern
* Low coordination? → slower, linear
* Athletic background? → dynamic

Client does **not** choose.
They experience personalization without decision fatigue.

⬇️

---

## NODE 5 — GLUTES, HIPS, FUNCTIONAL SPINE

🎥 *“These areas protect you later.”*

**Movements**

* Glute activation
* Hip mobility
* Spine flex/extend/rotate (controlled)
* Golf-style rotation

**Decision check**

* Discomfort?
* Confusion?
* Asymmetry?

**Branch**

* Any issue → extra cue video unlocks
* No issue → proceed normally

⬇️

---

## NODE 6 — SUPERSET 1 (DAY 1 = UPPER BODY)

🎥 *“This is how we train without rushing.”*

**Explanation**

* Why supersets
* Why upper body today
* Why tempo > weight

**Decision checks**

* Form breakdown?
* Breath holding?
* Overthinking?

**Branch**

* YES → coaching density stays high
* NO → reduce video interruptions later

⬇️

---

## NODE 7 — SUPERSET 2 (UPPER BODY CONT.)

🎥 *“Same movements, better awareness.”*

**Focus**

* Reinforce cues
* Smooth transitions
* Consistency

**Decision check**

* Confidence improving?

  * YES → Confidence +1
  * NO → Confidence unchanged

⬇️

---

## NODE 8 — SUPERSET 3 + CORE EDUCATION

🎥 *“Core is not abs. This is why your back stays safe.”*

**Explain**

* What core actually is
* Why it shows up everywhere
* How this connects to daily life

**Decision check**

* Can they brace under fatigue?
* Any back discomfort?

**Branch**

* Issue → mark Core Awareness = low
* No issue → Core Awareness = adequate

⬇️

---

## NODE 9 — COOL DOWN + EXPECTATION SETTING

🎥 *“Here’s what you’ll feel, and why.”*

**Education**

* Why soreness shows up 24–48 hrs later
* Difference between soreness vs pain
* What *this* workout may cause
* Why 2 sets this week
* Why 3 sets later

This node dramatically reduces drop-off.

⬇️

---

## NODE 10 — DAY 1 EXIT STATE

🎥 *“You did exactly what you needed to do today.”*

**State saved**

* Confidence level
* Pain flags
* Form quality
* Coaching density recommendation for Day 2

**Outcome**

* Unlock Day 2
* Set tone for autonomy progression

* Videos feel intentional, not intrusive
* You’re teaching **how to train**, not just exercises

This is *exactly* how you scale real coaching.
